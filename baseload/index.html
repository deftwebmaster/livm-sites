Case Study

Regional 3PL turns accuracy crisis into competitive advantage

Industry: Third-Party Logistics
Facilities: 4
SKUs: 180,000+
Duration: 10 weeks

The Problem

A regional 3PL serving e-commerce and retail clients was bleeding margin to inventory discrepancies. Cycle counts were reactive, root cause analysis was nonexistent, and client chargebacks were climbing. Leadership suspected receiving and putaway errors, but had no data to confirm.

Our Approach

We embedded with the ops team for two weeks, mapping every transaction type and tracing 1,200 discrepancies back to origin. The data revealed that 68% of errors originated from a single workflow: partial pallet receiving with manual quantity entry.

We redesigned the receiving process to require scan verification at case level, implemented a daily discrepancy review cadence, and trained supervisors on root cause documentation.

The Result

Within eight weeks of go-live, the client achieved a sustained inventory accuracy rate above 99.2%—up from 94.1%. Cycle count labor dropped 40% as counts shifted from reactive wall-to-walls to targeted, exception-driven audits.
